The Godfather (1972)
The Godfather (1972) is a legendary crime drama directed by Francis Ford Coppola, based on Mario Puzo's novel. It chronicles the Corleone family, led by patriarch Vito Corleone (Marlon Brando), as they navigate a violent, shifting power structure in 1940s New York, transitioning control to his reluctant youngest son, Michael (Al Pacino).
